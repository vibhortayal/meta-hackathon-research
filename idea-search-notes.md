Raw notes from the open-ended "what idea can win" research. Not yet a write-up.

## What past Meta/Llama hackathon winners built
- Llama 3 hackathon (2024): 1st OpenGlass, $20 smart glasses that describe what you see. https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-3-hackathon-recap-cerebral-valley/
- LlamaCon hackathon (2025): 1st OrgLens (expert matching), 3rd Llama CCTV Operator, Best API use Geo-ML (geologist turning reports into 3D subsurface models). https://ai.meta.com/blog/llamacon-hackathon/
- Pattern: practical real workflows, domain experts, hardware/glasses demos.

## Leading candidate so far
Capture expert know-how from a video, then coach and verify a novice live (phone or Ray-Ban glasses).
- Commercial neighbours: SOPX, LeanSuite, Docsie (video to SOP authoring); Retrocausal (assembly mistake-proofing).
- Impact stat: up to 3.8M US manufacturing jobs needed by 2033, ~1.9M could go unfilled (Deloitte/Manufacturing Institute, Apr 2024).
- Uses Spark video, SAM 3.1 video, Voice Transcribe, Muse Image, Glimmer local, glasses DAT.
