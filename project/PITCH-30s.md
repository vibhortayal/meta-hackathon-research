# 30-second pitch (for friends' feedback) — 2026-10-27

Quick feedback ask — I'm building this for a hackathon: Target's app tells you the exact aisle; Trader Joe's and Costco tell you nothing, so you wander. My app builds the store map from shoppers' own videos, then guides you item to item with your camera. At checkout it photographs your receipt and cart, learns what you buy and how fast you use it, and writes your next list itself. Store moves something? You tap "not here" and it relearns. Honest answer: would you use it?
