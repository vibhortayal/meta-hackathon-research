## Overview

- The **Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon** is a virtual, worldwide, ten-day hackathon run by Meta (Meta Platforms, Inc.) and Cerebral Valley (Cerebral Valley Enterprises, Inc.), free to enter if selected (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- Tagline: "Come and build the future of superintelligence." Participants build with Meta's newest models for a share of a **$1,000,000** cash prize pool across two tracks — indie developers and startups (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- The event listing window is 2026-10-26T17:00:00Z to 2026-11-05T02:00:00Z, i.e. Oct 26, 1:00 PM ET to Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon). The Nov 4, 9:00 PM ET end of that window matches the submission deadline below.
- Participation is application-based and capped at **20,000 accepted applicants** (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- Two project themes apply across both tracks (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon):
  - **Build for Your World** — bring Meta's models into your own domain; solve a problem you know firsthand.
  - **Push the Frontier** — apply the models to software, robotics, manufacturing, energy, materials, and beyond.

## Eligibility & participation

- **Who can apply:** anyone, worldwide, subject to the exclusions below. Participants must be **at least 18 years old and the age of majority** in their jurisdiction of residence (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **Application required:** everyone must apply through the official application process and be accepted; submitting an application does not guarantee attendance. All fields must be completed accurately and truthfully — incomplete or false information may result in disqualification (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **Cap:** maximum 20,000 accepted applicants, reviewed on a rolling basis; the organizers may close the portal as soon as the cap is satisfied, even before the posted deadline (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- **How applications are reviewed** (event page): two criteria, weighted equally, judged only from your form answers —
  1. your project idea: how clear and feasible it is, and how well it fits one of the two themes;
  2. your technical skill, as shown in an AI project you describe. GitHub/LinkedIn/shipped-product links help verify experience but are **not required**.
  Applications meeting minimum scoring cut-offs are accepted automatically up to 20,000; others may be accepted by score if space allows. Answers to questions 13–16 are used to improve developer programs, **not** for selection (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- **Disqualification:** duplicate applications, false identity information, or spam, at the Sponsor's sole discretion (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- **Excluded** (both sources): countries where local law prohibits participation or receipt of an award (void where prohibited); anyone who is a target of US/EU/UN/UK trade sanctions or export controls, or acting on behalf of such a person; anyone organized or located in **Belarus, China, Russia, Ukraine, Syria**, or comprehensively sanctioned regions (**Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, Cuba, North Korea, Iran**); employees, contractors, and interns of Meta and Cerebral Valley; government-affiliated individuals; and those persons' immediate family (spouse, domestic partner, parents, legal guardian, legal ward, children, siblings, and their spouses) and household members (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- Selected participants must accept the Official Rules (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).

## Tracks

Every project enters exactly **one** track; a participant may apply for one track only, and a project may be entered in one track only (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).

- **Indie Developer track** — for individual builders and students; **teams of up to four (4)**. The event page says it is judged on creativity, technical ambition, and whether it works (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon). (The scored criteria in the Official Rules, below, apply to all projects.)
- **Startup track** — for teams and founders building a real product; judged on technical execution and whether it holds up in production (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- **Representative rule:** each team/startup must appoint one individual (the "Representative") who submits the project and acts for the team. The Representative must themselves be eligible, must be authorized to act for the team, and warrants that every member has read and agrees to the Official Rules. Indie Representatives must identify all other team members in the submission. A startup's Representative must be an active employee of the startup, authorized to receive notices and prize payment (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).

## Prizes & judging criteria

**Prize pool: $1,000,000 total anticipated** (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).

| Track | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | Track total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Indie Developer | $150,000 | $100,000 | $75,000 | $50,000 | $25,000 | $400,000 |
| Startup | $250,000 | $150,000 | $75,000 | $50,000 | $25,000 | $550,000 |

- **Bonus prizes:** the Organizers may award up to **$50,000 in the aggregate** for standout projects or community achievements — categories may include a community vote award or most viral project award; number, categories, allocation, and values are set by the Organizers before or during judging. Bonus prizes are announced on or about **November 10, 2026** and do not reduce the track prizes (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **Judging criteria for projects** (Official Rules; these apply to hackathon project submissions, not to applications) (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver):
  - **Impact (30%)** — real-world potential: who benefits, how much it matters, whether it could become something people use, fit with a problem statement/theme.
  - **Use of Meta AI Tooling (25%)** — how creatively Meta's AI tooling was used, going beyond a basic integration, surfacing unexpected capabilities.
  - **Demo (25%)** — a working, impressive demo; whether the product holds up live; and the **quality of the demo video**.
  - **Technical Depth & Execution (20%)** — sound, thoughtfully refined engineering; real craft.
- Finalists are chosen by a panel of judges selected by the Organizers; the judges' selections are final and binding. Prizes are awarded in USD, only after eligibility verification and compliance with the Official Rules (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).

## Rules, submission requirements & deliverables

Source: the Official Rules / Event Waiver (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver), which selected participants must accept.

The live form's exact fields sit behind participant sign-in; the text fetch of the portal returned the public event page, so verify the required fields while signed in (see `sources/submit-portal.md`).
- **Deliverables the rules imply:** a submitted project in one track; for teams, the Representative identified plus all other team members named in the submission (Indie track rule); and — because the Demo criterion (25%) explicitly scores "the quality of the demo video" and whether "the product holds up live" — a working demo and a demo video. The rules do not publish a separate checklist of required files/formats on the pages fetched; check the portal.

## Tools & models

From the event page (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon):

- **Muse Spark series** and **Muse Wonder**, **Muse Image**, and **SAM 3.1**, accessed through the **Meta Model API**.
- **Meta open-weights models**, runnable yourself or through the inference partners (Baseten, Modal, Fireworks) — the partner credits are specifically for using these open-weights models.
- **Muse Code**, Meta's coding agent, to "code faster."
- **Muse Spark Open:** the event pages do not publish specifications (parameters, context window, license terms) for a distinct "Muse Spark Open" artifact; on these pages, the open-weights offer is described as "run Meta open weights models" and the Baseten listing names the model ID `meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026` (per the Baseten credit form circulated to participants). Do not assume specs beyond what the pages state — check the Meta Model API docs / Baseten model page for the model's actual card before building against it. (Inference from the pages only: partner credits + "open weights models" + the form's model ID together indicate Muse Spark Open is the open-weights model the partner credits are for.)
- **Modal tooling worth knowing** (from the linked docs): Endpoints deploy open models as OpenAI-/Anthropic-compatible APIs (shared = per-token billing; dedicated = isolated capacity, scale-to-zero) (https://modal.com/docs/guide/endpoints); serverless GPUs span T4 through B300, up to 8 per container (https://modal.com/docs/guide/gpu); Sandboxes are secure containers for untrusted/LLM-generated code, 5-minute default lifetime up to 24 hours (https://modal.com/docs/guide/sandboxes); training quickstart centers on Modal Dojo for SFT/RL post-training (https://modal.com/docs/guide/training-quickstart).

## FAQ

No page titled "FAQ" was linked from the event pages as fetched on 2026-10-27. These answers are compiled strictly from the event page and the Official Rules, cited per answer.

- **What does it cost to enter?** Free if selected — "free to enter if selected," tickets $0; no purchase necessary and a purchase won't increase your chances of winning (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon; https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **Can anyone in the world join?** Almost — but not residents/organizations of Belarus, China, Russia, Ukraine, Syria, Crimea, Donetsk, Luhansk, Cuba, North Korea, or Iran; not sanctions targets; not Meta/Cerebral Valley staff, government-affiliated individuals, or their families/households; and not where local law prohibits it (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- **How big can my team be?** Indie track: teams of up to four. Every team needs one Representative who submits (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **Can I enter both tracks?** No — one track per participant and per project (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **How are projects scored?** Impact 30%, Use of Meta AI Tooling 25%, Demo 25% (including demo-video quality and how the product holds up live), Technical Depth & Execution 20% (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **When is the submission deadline, and where do I submit?** Nov 4, 2026, 9:00 PM ET, via https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/hackathon/submit (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon; https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **Do I get API credits?** Selected participants get $150 in Meta Model API credits for a 10-day development window (read the Credit Terms before claiming). Baseten ($100 + 2M TPM), Modal ($100), and Fireworks ($25) credits go to select participants only, by application/review (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver; https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon).
- **Who owns my project?** You do — but you grant the Organizers a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use and exploit the Submission for any purpose, and anything you share at the event is treated as non-confidential (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **How and when do winners get paid?** In USD via Tremendous bank payout, administered by Cerebral Valley, within 30 days of verification + tax form (W-9 or W-8BEN); winners bear taxes and transfer fees (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
- **When are winners announced?** Live finals and winners at the Nov 9 closing ceremony; bonus prizes on or about Nov 10, 2026 (https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon; https://cerebralvalley.ai/e/meta-ai-developer-hackathon/waiver).
